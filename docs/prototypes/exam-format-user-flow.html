Passage A — Rethinking public space

Questions 1–3 refer to this passage.

Cities around the world are redesigning public spaces to support walking, cycling, and social activity. The movement accelerated when communities saw how quickly streets could adapt to changing public needs.

Advocates argue that these changes improve health and support local commerce. Critics, however, worry about access for older residents and the displacement of traffic into surrounding neighborhoods.

Successful projects tend to begin as temporary trials. Cities collect evidence, listen to residents, and adjust the design before making it permanent.
